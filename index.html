The show

Find Your Ultra is a podcast about how ordinary people keep going, long after the excitement wears off.

The host

I'm Vipin. I've spent 110+ episodes asking people who do genuinely hard things how they keep showing up.

None of them talked about talent. They talked about small promises, kept on ordinary days, for much longer than felt interesting.

So I stopped only interviewing it. The Last 100 is me doing it in the open, for the final hundred days of the year, and asking you to do it alongside me.
